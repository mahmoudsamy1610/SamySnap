Set up SamySnap

About 5 minutes · you only do this once

Connect SamySnap to your Google Drive

Google asks every app that saves to Drive to register once. You're going to make your own private registration. It's free, and only you can use it.

  • ✓ Free, no credit card
  • ✓ About 5 minutes
  • ✓ Only once, then just Record → Stop → paste the link
How it works: each step has a button that opens the right Google page in a new tab. Do what the step says, then come back to this tab and click Next. Whenever you need to type something, there's a Copy button.

Step 1

Create your free Google Cloud space

This is a private space where your SamySnap registration lives.

Open Google Cloud ↗
  1. If Google asks you to accept the terms, choose your country, tick the box and click Agree and continue.
  2. In Project name, paste:
  3. Click Create and wait a few seconds.

Step 2

Allow Google Drive

This lets your space save videos to Drive.

Open the Google Drive page ↗
  1. At the top of the page, check that it says SamySnap. If it doesn't, click the name there and pick SamySnap.
  2. Click the blue Enable button.

Already says “API Enabled” or shows a Manage button? Then this step is done.

Step 3

Name your app

This is the name Google shows when you connect.

Open the app settings ↗
  1. Click Get started.
  2. App name: and for User support email, pick your email. Click Next.
  3. Audience: choose External, then click Next.
  4. Contact information: type your email, then click Next.
  5. Tick “I agree…”, then click Continue and Create.

If you see a page with a Create OAuth client button instead, this step is already done. Click Next here.

Step 4

Add yourself as a user

Only people on this list can connect, and that's just you.

Open the user list ↗
  1. Scroll down to Test users and click + Add users.
  2. Type the Gmail address you'll connect with.
  3. Click Save.

Step 5

Create your SamySnap key

Google gives you a key that lets SamySnap sign in. You'll paste it in the next step.

Open “Create client” ↗
  1. Application type: choose Web application.
  2. Name:
  3. Scroll to Authorized redirect URIs, click + Add URI and paste this link: Use the second box (“redirect URIs”), not “JavaScript origins”.
  4. Click Create.
  5. A box shows your Client ID, a long text ending in .apps.googleusercontent.com. Click the copy icon next to it.

Closed the box too early? Open your clients and click SamySnap to see the Client ID again.

Step 6

Paste your key and connect

Paste it with Ctrl+V (⌘V on Mac).

What you'll see next: a Google window opens.
  1. Pick your account.
  2. Google says “Google hasn't verified this app”. That's normal, because it's your own app. Click Continue.
  3. Allow access and click Continue.

🎉

You're all set!

SamySnap is connected to your Google Drive.

  1. Click the SamySnap icon in the toolbar, then Record. Can't see it? Click the 🧩 puzzle icon and pin SamySnap.
  2. Pick Entire screen, a window or a tab, then click Share.
  3. Click Stop & copy link and paste the link anywhere.

Shortcut: Alt+Shift+R starts and stops recording.

Optional: stop Google asking you to sign in again every week

While your app is in “Testing”, Google asks you to reconnect about once a week. To stop that:

  1. Open the user list page again.
  2. Click Publish app, then Confirm.

It stays private. Google doesn't need to review it, because SamySnap only touches files it creates.

Videos are saved in your Drive in a folder called Bug Recordings.

Having trouble?
Google says “Access blocked” or “has not completed the Google verification process”
Your email isn't on the user list. Do step 4 again with the exact Gmail you're connecting with.
Google shows “Error 400: redirect_uri_mismatch”
The link in step 5 wasn't pasted exactly. Open your client, fix the link under Authorized redirect URIs, click Save, wait a minute and try again.
Google shows “Error 401: invalid_client”
The pasted key isn't right. Copy the Client ID again (not the “Client secret”) and paste it in step 6.
Google asks for a billing account or credit card
You don't need one for SamySnap. Close that page and continue with the next step.
I can't find a button the guide mentions
Google sometimes moves things around. Check that SamySnap is selected at the top of the Google page, then look for the words in the guide.
